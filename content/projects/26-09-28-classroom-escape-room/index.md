**Can you crack the code and find your way out?** MURGILDU took part in the **Godot XR Game Jam — September 2026** with *Classroom Escape Room*, a small educational VR prototype built in Godot.

The idea is simple: explore a professor's office, follow clues, and use interactive machines to recover an encrypted code. The machines handle the calculations; your challenge is to work out what comes next. It's an introduction to **RSA cryptography** through exploration and puzzles.

**[Urko Mungia]({{< relref "/authors/urko-mungia" >}}) led the project and carried out most of the development**, with mentoring from [Inigo Lopez-Gazpio]({{< relref "/authors/inigo-lopez" >}}) and an original cryptography idea from [Andoni Mujika]({{< relref "/authors/andoni-mujika" >}}).
